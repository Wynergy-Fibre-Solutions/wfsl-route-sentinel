\# Verification



Verification confirms deterministic route enforcement behaviour.



\## Verification intent

\- Undeclared routes are denied

\- Declared routes are explicitly allowed

\- Behaviour is reproducible



\## Verification scope

This verification does not assert:

\- security guarantees

\- performance guarantees

\- external system correctness



\## Minimum verification requirements

WFSL verification requires:

1\. Apache License 2.0 detected

2\. Governance artefacts present

3\. Deterministic verification runner passes

4\. Evidence artefact committed
