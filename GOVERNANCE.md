\# Governance



WFSL Route Sentinel enforces explicit route-level execution boundaries.



\## Scope

\- Applies only to explicitly declared routes

\- No inference or auto-discovery

\- Deny-by-default execution



\## Non-claims

\- No security guarantees

\- No compliance guarantees

\- No runtime correctness beyond route enforcement



\## Change control

\- Routes must be explicitly declared

\- Removal preferred over expansion

\- Behavioural changes require re-verification



\## Elevation readiness

Eligible for WFSL verification when:

\- Apache 2.0 licence is present

\- Verification artefact exists

\- Deterministic verification runner passes
